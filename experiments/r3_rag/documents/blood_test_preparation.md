# Blood Test Preparation

Patients preparing for a fasting blood test should fast for 8 hours before their appointment, when instructed by their healthcare professional.

During the fasting period, patients should follow the specific instructions provided by their doctor or clinic.

Patients should bring their identification card when visiting the clinic for a blood test.

Patients should arrive 15 minutes before their scheduled appointment.

If patients have questions about their medications before a blood test, they should contact their healthcare professional. They should not stop prescribed medication without medical advice.
