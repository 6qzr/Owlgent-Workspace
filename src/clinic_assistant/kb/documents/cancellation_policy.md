# Appointment Cancellation Policy

Patients should cancel or reschedule their appointments at least 24 hours before the scheduled appointment time.

Patients can contact the reception desk during clinic operating hours to request a cancellation.

When requesting a cancellation, patients should provide their name and appointment details.

Appointments that are cancelled can be rescheduled based on available appointment slots.

Patients who cannot attend their appointment are encouraged to inform the clinic as early as possible.
