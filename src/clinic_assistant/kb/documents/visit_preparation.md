# Preparing for Your Clinic Visit

Patients should arrive at least 15 minutes before their scheduled appointment.

Patients must bring a valid national ID or identification document.

Patients with health insurance should bring their insurance card.

Patients should bring any relevant previous medical reports or test results.

Patients should prepare a list of their current medications and inform the doctor about any known allergies.

Patients should contact the clinic if they need special assistance during their visit.
